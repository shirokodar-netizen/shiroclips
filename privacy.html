Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What we access

When you connect your TikTok account, ShiroClips requests only: your basic profile (display name), to show which account is connected, and permission to publish videos that you choose to post (video.publish).

Where data is stored

ShiroClips runs entirely on your computer. Access tokens are stored only on your device, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. We do not operate servers that receive your data, and we do not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties.

Videos

Videos are processed locally on your computer and sent only to the platforms you choose, through their official APIs.

Your choices

You can disconnect your account at any time in the app (Publish tab) and revoke access in your TikTok settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the local tokens.

Contact

Questions: contact the ShiroClips team through the support channel provided with your license.

Política de Privacidade (PT-BR)

Ao conectar o TikTok, o ShiroClips pede apenas o perfil básico (nome de exibição) e a permissão de publicar os vídeos que você escolher. O app roda inteiro no seu computador: os tokens ficam só no seu PC, criptografados (Windows DPAPI). Não temos servidores que recebam seus dados e não vendemos nem compartilhamos dados com terceiros. Você pode desconectar a conta no app a qualquer momento.
